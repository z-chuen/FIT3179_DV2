Everyday Household Products in Malaysia

Compare the household products included in a published Shopee Malaysia sample, then examine observed prices of named cleaning products across Malaysian shops. Each source describes its own sample and observation period.

Prices and customer feedback

These listings were observed in 2023. Their prices and ratings describe that historical sample. Listing sizes, variations and set contents differ.

02 · Dot plot

Every dot is one listing. Dots with the same score are stacked vertically so all five source category groups can be shown together. The legend distinguishes cleaning, storage and kitchen products.

03 · Scatter plot

Hover over a point to see its listing and recorded score. Click a legend entry to highlight a group. Equal horizontal distances represent equal price ratios. The rating axis is zoomed to 4–5, and repeated scores naturally form horizontal bands. One missing price is excluded.

04 · Box plot

A box contains the middle half of listing prices, with the median marked inside. Whiskers follow the standard 1.5-IQR rule; separate dots show prices outside them. The logarithmic axis retains unusually high and low source prices. These are per-listing prices, not standardised unit prices.

05 · Heatmap

Each row totals 100%, making categories with different numbers of listings comparable. Cell tooltips show both percentages and counts. Displayed labels are rounded.

06 · Waffle chart

Each square represents approximately 1% of the listings. Exact calculated sample shares are shown in the legend. Abbreviated source counts such as “1.2k” have limited precision. Ratings and rating counts do not establish verified sales.

Price differences across Malaysia

Select the same product and pack throughout each map. The maps include all 13 states and three federal territories. Statistics describe sampled shops and are not weighted to represent every shop in a state.

09 · Choropleth map

State colour represents the median observed pack price. Grey states have fewer than five sampled outlets or no observations; hover to check coverage. Five outlets is a display threshold, not a guarantee of representativeness.

10 · Proportional-symbol map

Circle area represents the difference between the upper and lower quartiles, or the price range across the middle half of sampled shops. A small hollow marker means zero spread. State-level display anchors do not identify individual shop locations.

Coverage behind the comparisons

The number of observed shops matters when interpreting differences. More dots mean more sampled outlets for that product, not more purchases or more shoppers.

11 · Dot-density map

One dot represents one distinct sampled outlet in its state. Positions are generated within state boundaries for this aggregated dot-density display. They are explicitly illustrative, not geocoded shop coordinates.
